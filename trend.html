Is it getting better?

The learning loop, measured — not asserted.
Provably getting smarter (walk-forward)

The system replays its own rules with no peeking at the future: it landed 55.4% early and 59.5% later across 5326 replayed matches in 139 leagues — it has improved by +4.11 points as evidence accumulates.

Verifying its own confidence

1 confidence band had enough finished matches to be checked, and the published probabilities are corrected where the system was systematically off.

Pricing what it cannot see

A data-blindness tax (no live soccer injury feed, no prior meeting for most pairs) is applied to every published probability, so a data-poor league is never over-priced.

Adapting per league

132 leagues carry an adaptive pick floor, 19 a tuned recipe and 156 a recursive strength model — each derived from its own results, from a fixed base so it cannot ratchet.

Watching for drift

4 leagues currently shows a changed goals-per-match profile and has its goal rates rescaled.

Closing-line value — is the price any good?

The market's grade of our price, not the scoreboard's.
-0.51ppMean CLV across 21 priced picks
3/21Opened better than the close

Hit rate can be variance; CLV is the market grading our price. Positive means we consistently took a better number than the closing line. This is the forward-looking skill signal and is deliberately tracked separately from results.

Every probability here is priced with a data-blindness tax: we have no live injury feed for soccer (−2pp on the favourite) and no prior meeting between most pairs (−1pp). These are league-wide conditions so they are stated once here rather than repeated under every match; they still reduce every published win probability.
