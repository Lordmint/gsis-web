How pick settlement works

Results come from the ESPN scoreboard for the picked competition/week (final + goal detail timeline) with a local DB fallback. Settled at a flat £1 stake using the card's published price: W = +odds−1, L = −1, push AH = 0. 1UP/2UP are settled from the actual goal sequence; when no timeline exists they stay PENDING rather than guess. Hit-rate counts only settled picks. Data in api/card_results.json.
