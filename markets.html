What the markets mean

  • 1X2 — back Home, Draw or Away on the 90-minute result.
  • 1X2 · 1UP / 2UP — early-payout: the bet settles as a win the moment the backed team takes a 1- or 2-goal lead, whatever happens later.
  • Double Chance — the team wins or draws (1X at home / X2 away); only the opposite result loses.
  • Double Chance 1UP — same draw protection, plus early settlement on any 1-goal lead taken at any point.
  • Home/Away or GG — the team wins or both teams score; only a clean-sheet loss for the other side cancels the bet.
  • Over / Under — total goals relative to the 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.5 lines (supporting pick).
