How to read this

Modelled edge = average of (model prob × odds − 1). Realised edge = average profit per settled pick at £1. A positive modelled edge that the realised edge tracks is the whole point of the weekly card. Calibration buckets compare the model's stated probability with actual outcomes, but at this N every bucket is statistical noise — the 90% credible interval (Beta posterior, uniform prior) says how far the true rate could realistically sit from what we've seen so far. A bucket whose realized rate falls below the interval is "over-implied" (the model overstates wins — dangerous), above it "under-implied" (the model understates them). The bankroll chart stacks the same settled picks under three staking policies; Bayesian ¼-Kelly shrinks the stake when the probability is uncertain. Drift is an EWMA on goals per completed match (last 60 vs the 160 before), from the local match database. Data in api/backtest.json.
