For each match in the published week's feed, the better-placed side is checked against four rules — it must be strictly better in the table, with at most one loss or one draw (not both) across its last 3 recorded results, it must have won at least higher season win share over 5+ league games, and it has stronger rating than the opponent. It must have at most one loss or one draw (not both) in its last 3 recent results. For direct H2H it needs at least 3 meetings with at least two recent wins (or a win and a draw in recent meetings), more wins than losses overall, and at most one recent loss or one recent draw (not both). Thin or missing direct H2H is research-only; there is no fallback favourite (a stronger-form side with the worse table position does not become the favourite — the position rule is part of the definition).

Head-to-head is the first gate: a pair with fewer than 3 direct meetings is shown with a limited-evidence warning. Comparable-opponent history may be shown as proxy research, but it never replaces direct meetings. Zero table/form data remains a hard stop.

What it is not: this is a screening + market-suggestion surface only. It reads no odds, no EV, no stake, and feeds no card gate, floor, probability or pick count — the weekly card and the pick gate are untouched by construction. The suggested vehicle (Favourite to win or GG) is a dated research note, not a per-match probability: research note (2026-10, 58k+ settled matches): this vehicle held 77% for better-placed sides.
