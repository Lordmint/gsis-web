What this page is (and is not)

For each match in the published week's feed, the better-placed side is checked against four rules — it must be strictly better in the table, unbeaten across at least its last 3 recorded results (a draw is fine, a loss is not), it must have won at least 72% of its 5+ league games this season, and it must show the SAME characteristic against the team it is about to play: it must NOT have lost the MOST RECENT meeting with that opponent, over at least 3 prior meetings. All four must hold; there is no fallback favourite (a stronger-form side with the worse table position does not become the favourite — the position rule is part of the definition).

Missing head-to-head is a rejection, not a pass: a pair with fewer than 3 prior meetings on record — including a cross-division or newly met pair with none — fails the screen by rule, because there is no opponent-specific evidence to judge. Zero data is a hard stop, exactly as the pick gate treats a missing form window.

What it is not: this is a screening + market-suggestion surface only. It reads no odds, no EV, no stake, and feeds no card gate, floor, probability or pick count — the weekly card and the pick gate are untouched by construction. The suggested vehicle (Favourite to win or GG) is a dated research note, not a per-match probability: research note (2026-10, 58k+ settled matches): this vehicle held 77% for better-placed sides. Head-to-head lines are informational evidence from prior meetings only.

Every probability here is priced with a data-blindness tax: we have no live injury feed for soccer (−2pp on the favourite) and no prior meeting between most pairs (−1pp). These are league-wide conditions so they are stated once here rather than repeated under every match; they still reduce every published win probability.
