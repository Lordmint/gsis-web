What this page is (and is not)

For each match in the published week's feed, the better-placed side is checked against four rules — it must be strictly better in the table, have at most one loss or one draw (not both) in its recent form window (at least 3 results, up to 5), hold a season win share of at least 72% over 5+ league games, and be stronger in the scored snapshot than the opponent. Head-to-head is then the first qualification: fewer than 3 prior meetings qualifies only when the favourite won all of them; 3 or more requires more wins than losses and at most one non-win (one draw or one loss, not both) that is not the most recent meeting. There is no fallback favourite (a stronger-form side with the worse table position does not become the favourite — the position rule is part of the definition).

Head-to-head is the first gate: a pair with fewer than 3 direct meetings is shown with a limited-evidence warning. Only pure direct meetings count — there is no proxy or comparable-opponent substitute. Zero table/form data remains a hard stop.

Market picture: live screened-in cards carry a 📊 market read (model): built from the same goal grid the market pages use; archived rows keep their frozen vehicle and H2H because the grid is not stored in the weekly archive. It remains a display read only.

What it is not: this is a screening + market-suggestion surface only. It reads no odds, no EV, no stake, and feeds no card gate, floor, probability or pick count — the weekly card and the pick gate are untouched by construction. Head-to-head lines are informational evidence from prior meetings only.

The market is chosen per match, from that fixture's own goal record (the favourite's home games and the opponent's away games, never the season average): a clear venue winner (won 60%+ of its venue games, by 25% over the opponent, at most 1 venue defeat) gets win or draw; a clear winner whose opponent scores in almost every game while the favourite rarely keeps a clean sheet gets GG; and a match with no clear winner gets an over/under on the goal total, choosing the line per match from 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.5 — the ladder is tried outward from the pair's expected total, and at the first supported line the side the sample backs is taken (over when it held in 55%+, under when it held in 55%+; the protective win or draw when the total is split). A clear winner whose opponent almost never scores still gets win or draw. It is a count-based research read, not a per-match probability: research note (2026-10, 58k+ settled matches): the win-or-GG vehicle held 77% for better-placed sides. Watch and expanded-research rows carry the same read as research context only — they are never screen-ins and still carry no odds, EV or stake. Expanded research additionally requires the opponent to have lost at least 3 of its last 5 current-league matches, and is shown with its own evidence, H2H line and model market read.
