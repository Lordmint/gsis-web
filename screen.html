What this page is (and is not)

For each match in the published week's feed, the better-placed side is checked against four rules — it must be strictly better in the table, have at most one loss or one draw (not both) in its recent form window (at least 3 results, up to 5), hold a season win share of at least 72% over 5+ league games, and be stronger in the scored snapshot than the opponent. Head-to-head is then the first qualification: fewer than 3 prior meetings qualifies only when the favourite won all of them; 3 or more requires more wins than losses and at most one non-win (one draw or one loss, not both) that is not the most recent meeting. There is no fallback favourite (a stronger-form side with the worse table position does not become the favourite — the position rule is part of the definition).

Head-to-head is the first gate: a pair with fewer than 3 direct meetings is shown with a limited-evidence warning. Only pure direct meetings count — there is no proxy or comparable-opponent substitute. Zero table/form data remains a hard stop.

What it is not: this is a screening + market-suggestion surface only. It reads no odds, no EV, no stake, and feeds no card gate, floor, probability or pick count — the weekly card and the pick gate are untouched by construction. The suggested vehicle (Favourite to win or GG) is a dated research note, not a per-match probability: research note (2026-10, 58k+ settled matches): this vehicle held 77% for better-placed sides. Head-to-head lines are informational evidence from prior meetings only.
