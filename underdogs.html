Read this first — this page publishes no picks

Everything below is analysis. Nothing here is a recommendation, nothing is added to the weekly card, and nothing changes a gate, probability, EV or pick count.

The reason is measured, not assumed. We already have a head-to-head signal, and it works against the favourite: when a side we call a favourite has historically failed to beat one specific opponent, the pick loses a 8pp shave — and on the settled card those flagged slots realised 54.0% win / 23.2% loss against 62.0% for clean slots. So H2H does carry real information, but what we have proven is “this favourite is worse than it looks”, not “the underdog is a bet”. Those are different claims and only the first has evidence behind it.

Two honest limits on the H2H columns: most pairs have fewer than three meetings on record (shown anyway, because hiding them would hide the weakness), and H2H carries no age decay — a six-year-old meeting counts exactly like last season's.
