What this page is

The gate inspects every fixture with exactly one Elite/Reliable side against an Unranked one. This week that was 197 fixtures: it cleared all of its gates on 64 and declined 133. Every declined fixture appears below, bucketed by why, with the modelled probability printed next to the floor it missed..

"Cleared every gate" is a fixture-level count and not a list of published bets: the weekly card additionally requires a verified bookmaker price with non-negative EV, and it lists one entry per team, so a fixture that clears the gate can still end up absent from the card. This page publishes no picks of its own — the 133 rows below are the gate's rejects, read straight from its own decision record, and this page does not re-derive or second-guess the rulebook.

Read this first — a reject is not a near-miss endorsement

Everything below is analysis. Nothing here is a recommendation, nothing is added to the weekly card, and nothing changes a gate, probability, EV or pick count.

A fixture priced at 69.5% against a 70% floor appears here because you asked what the gate turned down — not because 69.5% beats the floor. That floor is 0.70 because gated picks realised 65.4% at their best, and this page is the instrument for noticing when the floor is wrong, never a licence to publish underneath it. Fixtures marked not priced were never given a probability at all: the gate refuses to price a fixture when data is missing, which is a data problem and not a verdict on the match.

Head-to-head columns carry two honest limits: most pairs have fewer than three meetings on record (shown anyway, because hiding them would hide the weakness), and H2H carries no age decay — a six-year-old meeting counts exactly like last season's.
